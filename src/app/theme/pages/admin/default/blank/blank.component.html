about the ERA app

ERA is a new, high-performance and easy to use job application app. The app is developed focusing on the job advertisement and easy application features.

  • Advertisement

  • Networking

  • Track

  • Endorsement

features of ERA

  • Job Advertisement

    Career progress & opportunities made available anytime, anywhere.

  • Career Profiling

    With automated integration to your employee profile, ERA can be personalized to display your previous achievements. Declare your skills and get ready to be endorsed by your colleagues.

  • Skills Endorsement

    Get endorsed for your skills and get to know who endorsed you. Each endorsement is a testimony to your capabilities and a catalyst towards your career progression.

  • Application Tracking

    No more guessing on the status of your job applications. You can track the status directly in app anytime, anywhere.

  • Build Networking

    ERA makes it easy for you to connect with friends and colleagues who knows you best. ERA allows you to connect with mutual friends within a unique network.

  • 24/7 Available

    Window to your future career, made available anytime, anywhere.
