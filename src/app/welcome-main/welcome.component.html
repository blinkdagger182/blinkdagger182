features of ERA

  • Career Profiling

    Get endorsed for your skills and get to know who endorsed you. Each endorsement is a testimony to your capabilities and a catalyst towards your career progression.

    ERA can be accessed at any time of the day; whether to explore new opportunities or simply to enjoy the many functionalities that comes with it. We are also continuously working on developing additional features, so stay tuned!

    With ERA, users no longer need to waste their time on guessing the status of job applications. ERA also provides you real-time updates on new job postings for you to easily apply when you are ready to take the challenge.
    *available in ERA App.

    ERA makes it easy for you to connect with friends and colleagues who knows you best. ERA allows you to connect with mutual friends within a unique network.
